Bible questions, answered from Scripture

Answers follow the 28 Fundamental Beliefs of the Seventh-day Adventist Church and always cite the Bible. For personal matters, talk to your pastor.

What happens when a person dies?

The Bible describes death as a sleep: the dead are unconscious and rest until Jesus returns. At His second coming the righteous dead are raised to eternal life.

Ecclesiastes 9:5 John 11:11–14 1 Thessalonians 4:16
Fundamental Belief 26 · Death and Resurrection

The Sabbath · Lesson 2 of 6

A day made holy

God did not only create the world; He set apart a time to meet with us in it. The fourth commandment begins with “remember”, pointing back to creation and forward to a weekly rhythm of rest and worship.

Read also Genesis 2:1–3
